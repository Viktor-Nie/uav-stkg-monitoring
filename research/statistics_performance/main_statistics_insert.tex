For the language results, we additionally report conditional resampling intervals (Supplementary Section~\ref{supp:sec:conditional-uncertainty}). The English analysis resamples the eight observed source families together across methods. The LLM minus character-SVM difference in equal-family fixed-three-class F1 is 0.056, with a 95\% conditional percentile interval of $[-0.068,0.157]$. On the nineteen Chinese texts, source-family and saved-context resampling give substantially overlapping intervals across the four prompt conditions. These are sensitivity descriptions conditional on the curated families, saved predictions, and provisional source-reviewed reference; they are not confidence intervals for a randomly sampled requirement population or evidence of general model superiority. Repeated contexts and overlapping source documents do not increase the number of independent texts or flights.
